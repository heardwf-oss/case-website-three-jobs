The project

The first phase of the Noss on Dart redevelopment provides a floating marina, boatyard, dry stack, parking and tenant buildings. The scheme reuses a historic marine site on the eastern bank of the River Dart.

Working below the water table

CASE’s specialist involvement included temporary works and design input to the foul-water pumping station, alongside structural input from Hamill Davies. The works used an 8 m × 8 m secant-piled cofferdam around a 7.5 m-deep wet-well excavation in tidal groundwater.

Controlled dewatering and coordination with the operational marina were central to construction. CASE also prepared calculations and details for stainless-steel cladding backing plates within the wider development.
