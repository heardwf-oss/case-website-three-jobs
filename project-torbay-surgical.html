The project

MW Benney appointed CASE to design temporary support for the removal of loadbearing walls as part of internal alterations at Torbay Hospital. The works involved forming and altering openings in the existing structure.

Supporting the existing structure

CASE designed temporary needle beams and props for the formation and alteration of openings and associated lintel or beam replacement. The temporary support arrangement was developed using permanent-works information supplied by Airey & Coles.

The site photographs show exposed structure and support arrangements recorded during the works in May 2026. CASE’s appointment was for the temporary support package.
