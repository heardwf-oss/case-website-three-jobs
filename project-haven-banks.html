The project

CASE provided civil and structural engineering for the new centre, commissioned by Devon County Council and NPS South West. Its teaching and activity spaces include climbing and watersports facilities, a 23 m tower and a 45 m canal pontoon.

Building beside the water

Steel frames supported on piles created flexible internal spaces on a narrow waterfront site. The works had to accommodate high groundwater and concrete anchor blocks belonging to the existing quay and canal walls.

The centre opened in July 2013. The published construction contract value was £5.6m.
