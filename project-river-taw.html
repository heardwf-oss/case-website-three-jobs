The project

CASE provided detailed structural design for Dyer & Butler’s Network Rail contract. The work included strengthening plates and stiffeners, bracing, replacement bolts, access grating and masonry repairs.

Access for the works

CASE also assisted with the development of temporary scaffold support. Independent beams spanning between the existing piers supported access during the repair works.

The project formed part of the practice’s railway engineering work in 2015.
