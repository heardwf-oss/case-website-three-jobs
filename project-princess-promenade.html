The project

CASE prepared the engineering design, details and tender documentation for the Phase 1 East Promenade works. Investigation found that the deck and secondary beams required replacement, while the main supporting beams and columns could be retained.

Retaining useful structure

Hydro-demolition protected existing reinforcement and limited dust beside the marina. New precast beams and deck units were tied together with in-situ concrete and a structural topping.

This case study covers the Phase 1 deck replacement published in 2013. The reported contract value was £2.5m.
