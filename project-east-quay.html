The project

East Quay brings a public arts programme, makers’ studios, a café and visitor accommodation to the harbourside. The development opened in September 2021, with two galleries, eleven studios and five individually designed accommodation pods.

CASE’s involvement

CASE’s appointment was a temporary works commission for Midas Construction. The project combines several building forms and materials within a compact waterfront development.

Architecture by Ellis Williams and Invisible Studio. Permanent structural engineering by Momentum. CASE’s role was within the construction-stage works.
