The project

CASE carried out detailed structural design for Dyer & Butler’s design-and-build contract for Network Rail. The works included a 124 m platform, retaining walls, foundations and precast platform beams and planks.

Alongside the live railway

The adjacent single line stayed operational during construction. CASE worked with the contractor on the foundation installation method, with the aim of minimising disruption to the existing track.

The station also incorporated drainage, access routes, a shelter, cycle facilities and passenger information. The published project value was £0.75m.
